SAT Prep Toolkit

Essential SAT Math Formulas

Crucial equations & rules that are NOT provided on the official SAT Math Reference Sheet. Master these for top-score performance!

Slope Formula Algebra
m = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1)

Calculates the steepness or rate of change between two points (x1, y1) and (x2, y2).

SAT Tip: Parallel lines have equal slopes (m1 = m2). Perpendicular lines have negative reciprocal slopes (m1 · m2 = -1).
Slope-Intercept Form Algebra
y = mx + b

The standard representation of a linear equation where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept (0, b).

SAT Tip: If given Point-Slope form y - y1 = m(x - x1), convert to slope-intercept for quick identification of intercept.
Midpoint Formula Geometry
M = ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2)

Finds the exact center point between two coordinate points.

SAT Tip: Simply calculate the average of the x-coordinates and the average of the y-coordinates.
Distance Formula Geometry
d = √[(x2 - x1)2 + (y2 - y1)2]

Calculates the straight-line distance between two points in a coordinate plane.

SAT Tip: This is just the Pythagorean Theorem (a2 + b2 = c2) in disguise! Draw a right triangle on the grid if you forget it.
Quadratic Formula Advanced Math
x = [-b ± √(b2 - 4ac)] / 2a

Solves for x in any quadratic equation in standard form: ax2 + bx + c = 0.

SAT Tip: Ensure the equation is set to equal 0 before extracting a, b, and c.
The Discriminant Advanced Math
D = b2 - 4ac

Determines the number and type of solutions for a quadratic equation.

D > 0: 2 Real Solutions
D = 0: 1 Real Solution
D < 0: 0 Real Solutions (2 Complex)
Vertex of a Parabola Advanced Math
x = -b / (2a)

Finds the x-coordinate of the vertex (and the axis of symmetry) for y = ax2 + bx + c.

SAT Tip: Plug the x-value back into the original equation to find the y-coordinate of the vertex (minimum or maximum value).
Vertex Form Advanced Math
y = a(x - h)2 + k

A quadratic form where (h, k) represents the explicit vertex of the parabola.

SAT Tip: Pay attention to signs! In (x - 3)2 + 5, the vertex is at (3, 5).
Sum & Product of Roots Advanced Math
Sum = -b / a  |  Product = c / a

Quickly finds the sum or product of the solutions of ax2 + bx + c = 0 without solving.

SAT Tip: Saves massive amounts of time on high-level polynomial questions.
Exponential Growth / Decay Data Analysis
y = a(1 ± r)t

Models growth (+r) or decay (-r). a is initial amount, r is percentage rate (decimal), t is time.

SAT Tip: For compound interest, use A = P(1 + r/n)nt where n is compounding frequency per period.
Percent Change Problem Solving
% Change = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100

Calculates percentage increase or decrease relative to the original value.

SAT Tip: Always divide by the original (Old) value, never the new value!
Circle Equation Geometry
(x - h)2 + (y - k)2 = r2

Equation of a circle in a coordinate plane centered at (h, k) with radius r.

SAT Tip: Be prepared to "complete the square" to put general circle equations into this standard form!
Arc Length & Sector Area Geometry
Arc = (θ/360)×2πr  |  Area = (θ/360)×πr2

Calculates proportional fraction of circumference or total area for central angle θ in degrees.

If θ is in Radians:
Arc Length = r · θ  |  Sector Area = ½ · r2 · θ
Basic Trigonometric Ratios Trigonometry
SOH - CAH - TOA

sin(θ) = Opp / Hyp
cos(θ) = Adj / Hyp
tan(θ) = Opp / Adj

Complement Rule: sin(x°) = cos(90° - x°). If sin(A) = cos(B), then A + B = 90°.
Pythagorean Identity Trigonometry
sin2(θ) + cos2(θ) = 1

Fundamental identity relating sine and cosine of any angle.

SAT Tip: Can be rewritten as sin2(θ) = 1 - cos2(θ) to simplify complex trigonometric expressions.
