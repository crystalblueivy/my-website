From Crystal

Geometry's already strong, so we're leaving it. Nearly all your lost points are in algebra — and that's the most teachable thing on the whole test.

Why, in a bit more detail

More than half your mistakes came in the first module, on questions rated easy or medium. That isn't a maths problem, it's a pace-and-care problem — which is the quickest kind to fix. Get that first module above 80% and the test opens up the harder second half, and that's what raises your score.

Advanced Math builds on the same skills as algebra, so it gets easier on its own once algebra lands. We'll come back to Problem Solving nearer the test.
